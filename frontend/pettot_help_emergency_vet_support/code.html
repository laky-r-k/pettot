Is This Normal or an Emergency?

Surgical recovery has natural waves of mild discomfort, fluid shifts, and medication drowsiness. Compare your pet's current state with the triaged indicators below.

Normal & Expected
Safe to rest

Typical post-operative biological healing. Requires peaceful resting space, hydration access, and continued routine medication.

check_circle Mild pinkness around incision

Light pink edges without widespread swelling or heat radiating from the suture line are a standard inflammatory response.

bedtime Prolonged sleep / Lethargy

Gabapentin and mild analgesics induce deep, heavy naps. As long as Milo wakes up willingly for water or snacks, this is healthy.

hiking Guarding leg during short walks

Pet favors or hovers the leg slightly while relieving themselves. Gentle joint stiffness improves after short resting intervals.

done_all Continue standard daily log at 8:00 PM
Watch & Note Signs
Log in app

Non-critical variations. Monitor for 2 to 4 hours. Note changes during the next scheduled check-in or request advice if persistent.

no_meals Skipping a single scheduled meal

Mild nausea can occur after pills. Offer a small spoonful of plain boiled chicken or bone broth before worrying.

water_drop Minor clear fluid droplet

A tiny speck of odorless clear or pale serum on the fur. Dab gently with sterile gauze without rubbing the incision.

volume_down Whining only when standing up

Brief vocalization during posture changes indicates mild stiffness. If vocalization subsides once standing, it is not an acute emergency.

Call Clinic Immediately
Immediate ER

Do not wait for standard clinic hours. Contact emergency triage staff directly if any of these four symptoms appear.

crisis_alert Gap or opening between stitches

Any visible dehiscence, exposed subcutaneous layer, or loose stitch thread requires immediate protection with clean gauze and direct transport.

sick Foul odor or cloudy discharge

Thick yellow, milky green, or smelly secretion points to acute bacterial infection requiring prompt culture and IV antibiotic adjustment.

water_voc Refusing water for > 12 hours

Risk of severe post-op dehydration and kidney strain. Check gums: if pale or sticky to touch, seek ER fluid support right away.

sentiment_very_dissatisfied Extreme restlessness or continuous crying

Pacing, inability to lie flat, rapid shallow breathing, or crying without relief implies breakthrough surgical pain needing intervention.
