# PetTot editorial redesign notes

## Visual intent
- Match the supplied reference: wide photographic banner, dark-to-warm gradient overlay, serif headline, small uppercase eyebrow, one visible CTA in a white action panel.
- Reuse the banner component for overview, check-in, photo review, and care contacts so the product feels like one coherent professional system.
- Use a photo-backed split welcome panel instead of a tilted thumbnail/gallery collage.
- Keep forms, medication ticks and check-in answers on calm light surfaces with visible labels and focus states.
- Use simple stroke icons in navigation, reduced decoration and consistent rounded corners.
- On mobile, stack banner text and action rather than overlaying the button on dense text.

## Functional detail
- The overview CTA starts a check-in.
- The photo banner CTA scrolls the user to the image-analysis controls.
- The care banner CTA scrolls to and focuses the locally prepared vet summary.
- The app remains explicit about demo mode, browser-only records and unvalidated model outputs.
